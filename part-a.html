OVERALL JUDGEMENT

Both sources are useful for different reasons. Ibn Shaddād gives a close view of Saladin from his own time, while Phillips can compare many sources and perspectives. Neither source is completely neutral, but using them together gives historians a stronger and more balanced understanding of Saladin.
