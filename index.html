What this assessment covers

Each part asks a different question about the same figure: what the evidence says, what Saladin actually did, and how far one source can be trusted on its own.

The thread running through all three parts

Both a witness who knew Saladin and a historian writing 800 years later are useful, and neither is neutral. Ibn Shaddād gives closeness; Phillips gives comparison. Used together they give a stronger and more balanced understanding of Saladin than either could on its own.
